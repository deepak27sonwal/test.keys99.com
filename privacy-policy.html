Keys99 is a property listing platform. To connect buyers and tenants with sellers, agents and developers, we have to handle some personal data. This policy sets out exactly what we collect, why, who sees it, and what you can ask us to do with it.

1. Who we are

Keys99.com ("Keys99", "we", "us") is operated by [COMPANY LEGAL NAME], registered at [REGISTERED ADDRESS], India.

For the purposes of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, we are the Data Fiduciary for the personal data described below, and you are the Data Principal.

2. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Name, email address and phone number, when you create an account or send an enquiry.
  • Property details you submit as a listing — address, price, photographs, RERA number and contact number.
  • Messages you send us, including through the chat assistant on the site.

Information collected automatically

  • IP address, browser type, device type and operating system.
  • Pages viewed, searches run and listings opened.
  • Properties you save to favourites, and listings you recently viewed, which are stored in your own browser.

We do not knowingly collect financial account details, government identifiers such as Aadhaar or PAN, or any special category data through this website.

3. How we use it

  • To show you listings and let you search, filter and save them.
  • To pass your enquiry to the seller, agent or developer responsible for a listing.
  • To create and secure your account.
  • To review listings submitted for publication, and to detect fraudulent or duplicate ones.
  • To improve the site — which searches return nothing useful, which pages fail.
  • To meet legal and regulatory obligations.

We do not sell your personal data.

4. Your consent

We process your personal data on the basis of the consent you give when you create an account, submit an enquiry or post a listing, and for the certain legitimate uses permitted under the Act.

You can withdraw your consent at any time by writing to our Grievance Officer below. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing already carried out, and may mean we can no longer provide parts of the service — for example, we cannot forward an enquiry without passing on your contact details.

5. Who we share it with

This is the part worth reading closely. When you send an enquiry about a property, we share your name, phone number and email address with the seller, agent or developer who posted that listing, so that they can contact you. That is the purpose of the enquiry, and it cannot be separated from it.

We also share data with:

  • Supabase — our database, authentication and file storage provider, which hosts the data described above.
  • [HOSTING PROVIDER] — which serves this website.
  • Law enforcement or regulators — where we are legally required to.

We do not share your data with advertisers.

6. Cookies and local storage

We use your browser's local storage to keep you signed in and to remember your favourites and recently viewed properties. That information stays on your device and is not transmitted to us.

  • Account data — for as long as your account is open, and for [N] months after you close it.
  • Enquiries — for [N] months, so that disputes can be resolved.
  • Listings — for as long as they are published, and in our records for [N] months afterwards.

When data is no longer needed for the purpose it was collected for, and no legal obligation requires us to keep it, we delete it.

8. Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you may:

  • Ask what personal data we hold about you and how it has been processed.
  • Ask us to correct data that is wrong, or complete data that is missing.
  • Ask us to erase your data, where we are not required to keep it.
  • Withdraw consent you previously gave.
  • Nominate someone to exercise these rights on your behalf if you die or become incapacitated.
  • Raise a grievance with us, and escalate to the Data Protection Board of India if you are not satisfied.

To exercise any of these, write to the Grievance Officer below. We will respond within [N] days.

9. Security

The site is served over HTTPS, passwords are stored hashed by our authentication provider, and access to listing and enquiry data is restricted by database-level security rules.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal data, we will notify you and the Data Protection Board as the Act requires.

10. Children

Keys99 is not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly process the personal data of children. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact the Grievance Officer and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top always reflects the current version, and material changes will be notified on the site or by email.
